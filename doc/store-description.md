### Terra Namp — In-Game Music Player

Play your own music right inside Terraria. Replace the vanilla soundtrack with whatever you want.

**Download from YouTube**
Paste a link, hit Download. The mod handles everything — yt-dlp, FFmpeg conversion, volume normalization. Multiple downloads at once with progress bars.

**Import local files**
MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, M4A — any common audio format. Import entire folders at once.

**Multiplayer**
One player picks a song — everyone hears it. Songs are transferred automatically. Prefetch system pre-loads upcoming tracks for seamless transitions. Admin panel lets the host control who can DJ.

**Soundpad**
Instantly play sound effects over the music. Separate volume control.

**Slowed + Reverb**
Real DSP slowdown effect. Synced across multiplayer.

**Fully customizable UI**
- Accent color, background color, opacity, blur, corner radius
- Real-time audio visualizer (bars or waveform)
- Draggable panel with persistent position
- Mini player mode
- Search, folders, play modes (sequential / shuffle / loop)

**Requires:** SilkyUIFramework
**Works on:** Windows, Linux, macOS
