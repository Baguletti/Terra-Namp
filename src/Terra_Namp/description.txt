[h1]Terra Namp — Music Player for Terraria[/h1]
Play music from YouTube or local files right inside the game. Listen together in multiplayer — and load up the soundpad with meme sounds for when your friend dies to another dart trap.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[h3]YouTube Download[/h3]
Paste a link, click Download. Handles yt-dlp, loudness normalization, and conversion to MP3 automatically.

[h3]Playlist Download[/h3]
Paste a YouTube playlist link — downloads all tracks into a folder named after the playlist. Shows overall progress and ETA.

[h3]Multiplayer Sync[/h3]
One player picks a song — everyone hears it.
[list]
[*] [b]Auto-Transfer:[/b] Songs are sent to other players in the background.
[*] [b]Prefetch:[/b] Upcoming tracks load in advance so there's no gap between songs.
[*] [b]DJ Mode:[/b] Host controls who can change music.
[/list]

[h3]Local Files[/h3]
Import MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, M4A — single files or entire folders.

[h3]Slowed + Reverb[/h3]
Real-time audio effect, synced across all players.

[h3]Soundpad[/h3]
Play sound effects over the music. Separate volume, draggable button grid.

[h2]UI & Visualizer[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Visualizer:[/b] Bars or Waveform mode.
[*] [b]Glassmorphism:[/b] Blur, opacity, colors, corner radius — all adjustable.
[*] [b]Mini-Player:[/b] Compact mode.
[*] [b]Draggable:[/b] Place the panel anywhere, position is saved.
[/list]
